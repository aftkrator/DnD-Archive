SUMMARY

The session began with our “mercenaries”—hardly heroes—wandering in literal circles through a swamp that clearly didn’t want them there. After losing most of their original caravan to mysterious nightly deaths, George, Grudskar, Folkass, and John Redcock decided that a creepy, abandoned cave was a much better place to sleep than the open marsh.

The cave, however, had other ideas. After Folkass lit up the entrance with magic, the party was swarmed by “Stingbats”—nasty insectoid creatures with scorpion tails and a thirst for blood. The fight was nearly the end of the line for Grudskar and Folkass, who both went down under the swarm. Only some quick-thinking herbalism from John Redcock kept them in the land of the living. After the dust (and bug guts) settled, they scavenged a serrated greatsword from a skeleton, which Grudskar took with a grin.

Their “luck” continued when a nine-foot-tall Troll came whistling home. The party attempted an ambush, but they quickly learned that Trolls don’t care much for being stabbed; the beast simply reattached its severed arms and kept swinging. In a tragic turn, Folkass was slammed into a wall and perished before his friends could get a healing potion down his throat. Just as things looked bleakest in the total darkness, John Redcock managed to light a flaming arrow and put it right through the Troll’s eye, finally stopping its regeneration and ending the threat.

Searching the Troll’s grisly leftovers, the party struck gold—literally. They found a gold-inlaid mace and a suit of magical black steel plate mail, which Grudskar immediately strapped on.

Deeper in the cave, the smell of burning flesh led them to a Goblin Shaman’s lab. They kicked in the door to find a human named Snafu being tortured with a hot poker. In a flurry of blades and arrows, they cut down the shaman, and Snafu—showing some surprising skill—disappeared in a puff of smoke to join the fray with a dagger. Snafu turned out to be a “shabby but fancy” fellow who had been snatched by a massive procession of goblins.

As the session wound down, George helped himself to magical black leather armor from the shaman’s hoard that lets him “feel” hidden enemies. With Folkass gone but Snafu found, the battered trio (plus their new friend) decided to camp out in the lab, hoping the twenty other goblins Snafu mentioned weren’t planning on returning for a midnight scuffle.
