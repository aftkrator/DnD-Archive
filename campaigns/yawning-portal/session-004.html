SUMMARY

Session 4 began in the wake of the party’s battle with a horde of skeletons as the heroes sorted through their hard-won spoils. Vlad Laurent was particularly preoccupied with a curious crystal whistle inscribed with the name Night Caller. He blew it several times, but to the party’s confusion, it made no sound at all—leading Vlad to suspect it might be intended for a silent ritual rather than mere entertainment.

With their kobold guide Meepo (who was becoming increasingly distracted by the Citadel’s dragon-themed architecture) leading the way, the heroes pushed into a fog-filled hallway. The silence of the ruins was shattered when two diseased giant rats lunged from the rubble. Meepo proved his worth as the self-proclaimed “Keeper of Dragons” by immediately headshotting one rat with a sling, while Lucas Aedansbane expertly sliced the other to pieces. Once Alejandro Perez and Arthur Blackthorn caught up, Arthur used his druidic expertise to examine the carcasses, determining that the rats weren’t sickly because of the environment, but suffered from congenital birth defects present from birth.

As they continued, a second wave of rats ambushed them near a large dragon statue. Vlad saw an opportunity to test Night Caller again, blowing the silent whistle over the fresh corpses. To the group’s shock, the severed remains stood up as undead thralls under Vlad’s command—dubbed “Ratboi 1” and “Ratboi 2.” The transformation was so startling that Lucas immediately stabbed one of the reanimated rats in a knee-jerk reaction, sparking a heated argument between Vlad (who viewed them as useful “vanguards”) and Alejandro (who was deeply wary of traveling alongside the undead).

Advancing toward the next chamber, Lucas noticed a faint Draconic inscription on a dry fountain: “Let there be death.” Vlad, disregarding the ominous warning, spoke the phrase aloud in Draconic. The fountain instantly exhaled a cloud of toxic green gas. While most of the party managed to duck away, Vlad took the brunt of the spray, though his black dragonborn heritage shielded him from the worst of the poison.

The party then pushed into a ransacked storage room (Area 30), a pivotal moment in the session marked by an oppressive smell of rotting meat rising from a floor covered in filth, old bones, and fur. A thorough investigation revealed several corpses: two goblins, a kobold, and a human male identified as Karakas the ranger—providing a somber confirmation of their mission to find the missing adventuring party from Oakhurst.

The somber mood was broken when three diseased giant rats and their matriarch—Guthash, known to the goblins as the “Bloated One”—emerged from their vile nest. Guthash, a monstrously swollen rat roughly six feet long, displayed terrifying speed by rolling a natural 20 on initiative. She immediately targeted and tore apart Vlad’s newly reanimated rat familiars, destroying Rat Boy 1 and Rat Boy 2 instantly.

The party retaliated with overwhelming force — Lucas drew his bow and unleashed a devastating Sneak Attack, dealing 18 damage and bursting a pus-filled boil on Guthash’s diseased skin. Arthur, wild-shaped into a dire wolf, bit one of the smaller giant rats and dragged it prone, while Alejandro crushed another with his mace. Meepo proved highly effective once again, hurling a flask of acid that completely dissolved the third rat. Closing in on Vlad, Guthash struck back, dealing damage and forcing a constitution save. Vlad responded with a brutal taunt and brought his great axe down in a powerful vertical slash. Infused with Divine Smite, the 18-damage blow split the rat matriarch cleanly in half.

With the matriarch slain, the party pushed deeper into the fortress, stumbling upon two goblin sentries camped by a fire. Lucas ended the first goblin’s life before it could even stand, but the second scrambled toward the darkness to sound an alarm.

Vlad intervened with a magical Command, forcing the goblin to grovel on the floor. Arthur leaped into action, using his dire wolf form to chase down and pin the terrified sentry. Meepo, not to be outdone, tossed a bag of live centipedes and crickets onto the goblin to keep it panicked and distracted. With the sentry pinned beneath a dire wolf’s paw and swarming with insects, the party is now prepared to interrogate their new prisoner and extract the remaining secrets of the Sunless Citadel.
