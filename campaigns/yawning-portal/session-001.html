Session 1 & 2

SUMMARY

The adventure began in the village of Oakhurst, where a group of intrepid heroes gathered to answer a desperate plea for help. Vlad Laurent arrived at the behest of Kerowyn Hucrele, whose children, Talgen and Sharwyn, had disappeared while investigating the Sunless Citadel a month prior. Vlad was also personally motivated to find his long-standing acquaintance, Sir Braford, who had accompanied the siblings.

Joining him were Alejandro Perez and Lucas Aedansbane, old war companions who once smuggled vital supplies past enemy lines. Alejandro was on a mission from his ministry to investigate the "unnatural apples" produced by the citadel, while Lucas, ever the man of business, was sent by his faction (the Zhentarim) to uncover the mystery behind the fruit and the local twig blights.

Completing the group were Arthur Blackthorn, a Moon Druid searching for a rogue druid called "the Outcast" to see if he was the gardener responsible for the blighted vegetation, and Draven Shadowveil, an escaped convict whose very weapon seemed to be whispering for him to delve into the dark.

The party’s entry into the Sunless Citadel was a bit literal for Vlad, who took a sudden trip through a trapdoor. Fortunately, he avoided the spikes and made short work of a giant rat that thought he looked like a snack.

After regrouping in a circular chamber filled with dead goblins, the party decided to try the "Dragon Door" to the south. Lucas put his skills to the test and came agonizingly close to success, but the door’s arcane reinforcement was too much, leaving him with a broken set of lockpicks and a bruised ego.

While Arthur occupied himself trying to win the heart of a local rat, the rest of the group realized they were at a literal dead end.

Deciding that the south was a bit too locked for their liking, they doubled back and headed north. This path led them into a steaming boiler room where they were immediately ambushed by several mephits. The battle was swift and the party emerged victorious, clearing the room and proving they could handle the citadel's magical guardians. With the mephits dealt with, the intrepid heroes stood ready to push further into the ruins to find the missing Hucreles and stop the Outcast.
