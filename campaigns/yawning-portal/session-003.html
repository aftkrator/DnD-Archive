SUMMARY

Session 3 began with the party sorting through the aftermath of their mephit skirmish in a steaming boiler room. While searching the area, Vlad Laurent took an unceremonious tumble into a rusted iron keg, only to find five tiny sapphires at the bottom. As the group divided the gems, Draven Shadowveil’s patron whispered a secret into his mind: these weren’t just coins, but components intended for summoning elementals.

Pushing deeper into the ruins, Lucas Aedansbane entered an irregularly shaped chamber where they discovered a whimpering, scarred kobold named Meepo. Between sobs, Meepo explained that he was the “Keeper of Dragons” and that “wretched goblins” had stolen his charge—a white dragon wyrmling named Calcryx. Arthur Blackthorn quickly stepped in to provide medical aid, though he was careful to clarify that his green-glowing magic was “not good berries”. Near a shattered metallic cage, the party found three white dragon scales, confirming the kobold’s story.

The party followed Meepo to the court of Yusdrayl, a posh-accented kobold leader seated upon a throne made of fallen masonry. Lucas, acting as the group’s “man of business,” struck a lucrative deal with her: in exchange for the party brewing poison for the kobold warriors, they were granted 10 gold pieces and two spell scrolls: Knock and Spider Climb. Yusdrayl also provided a crucial lead on Belak the Outcast, a rogue druid living in the “Twilight Grove” below who uses the goblins as servants to distribute his magical, unnatural fruit.

Eager to find the missing adventurers from Oakhurst, the group headed for the “back door” to goblin territory. Arthur put his druidic survival training to use, identifying human-sized footprints in the dust that confirmed the party they were looking for had passed through this way roughly a month ago. Along the passage, they found an ornate, diving dragon fountain. By speaking the Draconic command “Let there be fire,” Vlad coaxed the fountain into life, releasing a glowing red liquid identified as “distilled dragon breath”. Lucas carefully bottled the fluid into several vials to serve as potent weapons for the trials ahead.

The adventurers eventually reached a magically locked door that radiated a noticeable chill. Rather than solving the riddle inscribed on the wood, Lucas simply used his new Knock scroll to blow the way open. Inside was a silent sanctuary filled with five sarcophagi and an obsidian altar. Vlad’s curiosity got the better of him when he scratched a dragon carving on the altar, causing five skeletons to lunge from their coffins.

The battle was far more desperate than the “cute” appearance of the skeletons suggested. The undead managed to “jump” and grapple Vlad, pinning him down and hammering him until he was at death’s door with only one hit point remaining. Arthur cracked skulls with a magically infused quarterstaff, and Vlad ultimately ended the fight with a dramatic, last-stand cleave that shattered the remaining skeletons.

After the dust settled, the party turned the room upside down. Lucas found a hidden compartment containing a sapphire dragon statue and six peridot jewels. They also claimed a magical crystal whistle inscribed with the name Night Caller and an Everlasting Candle. In a final stroke of druidic fashion, Arthur tied the candle to his elk horns with a strip of leather, ensuring the party had hands-free light for the dark journey ahead. With their spirits high and pockets heavy, the party officially advanced to level 3.
