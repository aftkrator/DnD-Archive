Session 3

Summary

Session three began with the party doing what they do best: poking things they shouldn't. Vlad took a literal tumble into a boiler, only to find five sapphires worth 10 gp each. While everyone else saw treasure, Draven heard his patron explain that the gems were actually intended for summoning elementals.

Deeper in the citadel, Lucas encountered the distraught kobold Meepo, guardian of the stolen white dragon wyrmling Calcryx. Meepo guided the party to the kobold leader, Yusdrayl, where Lucas negotiated an alliance, earning gold, spell scrolls, and valuable information about Belak the Outcast.

The party explored deeper into the ruins, discovered evidence that the missing adventurers had passed through, activated a magical draconic fountain that produced bottled fire breath, and forced open a magically sealed sanctuary using a Knock scroll.

Inside the sanctuary they fought five animated skeletons in a difficult battle. Afterwards they uncovered a hidden cache containing jewels, a sapphire dragon statue, the magical crystal whistle Night Caller, and a Potion of Fire Resistance before reaching Level 3.

Primary Ongoing Quests

  • The Rescue Mission
    Continue searching for Talgen Hucrele, Sharwyn Hucrele, Sir Braford, and Karakas, whose trail leads toward goblin territory.
  • Retrieve Calcryx
    Rescue the stolen white dragon wyrmling and return it to Yusdrayl for a promised reward.
  • Investigate Belak
    Discover the source of the magical apples and twig blights by confronting Belak the Outcast.

Open Plot Points
