SUMMARY

Session three began with the party doing what they do best: poking things they shouldn't. Vlad took a literal tumble into a boiler, only to find five sapphires worth 10 gold pieces each. While the rest of the party saw coin, Draven started hearing voices; his patron informed him that these stones were actually intended for summoning elementals.

Moving deeper into the citadel, Lucas stumbled upon a whimpering, traumatized kobold named Meepo. Between sobs and puppy-dog eyes, Meepo explained that his charge—a white dragon wyrmling named Calcryx—had been snatched by "wretched goblins". Sensing an opportunity for profit, the party had Meepo lead them to the kobold "queen," Yusdrayl.

Yusdrayl’s court was a lesson in high-stakes diplomacy. Lucas, playing the part of a "man of business," managed to convince the kobolds that the party was a "weapon of mass destruction" they wanted on their side. He struck a deal to brew poison for the kobold warriors in exchange for spell scrolls of Knock and Spider Climb and some hard cash. The "queen" also gave them the lowdown on Belak the Outcast, a rogue druid living below who was responsible for the magical apples and the local twig blight infestation.

With Meepo as their reluctant guide, the party headed for the "back door" to goblin territory. Arthur flexed his survival skills by identifying human footprints in the dust, confirming that the missing party they were searching for had indeed passed this way about a month ago. Along the path, they found a dry fountain that Vlad coaxed into life by speaking Draconic; the "distilled fire breath" it spat out was quickly bottled by a very prepared Lucas.

The adventure hit a literal wall at a magically locked door inscribed with a riddle about rebuking the dead. Lucas didn't bother with a rebuttal, instead opting to use his new Knock scroll to blow the door open. Inside was a sanctuary filled with sarcophagi and an obsidian altar. Vlad, apparently tired of peace, scratched the dragon carvings on the altar, causing five skeletons to lunge out of their coffins.

The ensuing brawl was more intense than expected. The "cute" skeletons (as the DM called them) nearly turned Vlad into a permanent resident of the tomb after they jumped and grappled him. Arthur managed to crack a skull with his magically infused quarterstaff, and Vlad eventually ended the fight with a dramatic, one-HP-remaining cleave that shattered the last two enemies.

After the dust settled, the party turned the room upside down. They hit the jackpot in a hidden compartment, walking away with a sapphire dragon likeness, jewels, and a magical crystal whistle called Night Caller. In a final stroke of druidic fashion, Arthur tied an Everlasting Candle to his elk horns, ensuring the group wouldn't lose their way in the dark. With their bags full and their spirits high, the party officially leveled up to 3, ready to face whatever Belak and the goblins have in store.

Primary Ongoing Quests

  • The Rescue Mission: The party is searching for a group of missing adventurers who entered the citadel a month ago. This group includes Talgen Hucrele, Sharwyn Hucrele, Sir Braford, and Karakas. Arthur confirmed through a survival check that human-sized footprints—likely belonging to this party—pass through the citadel toward goblin territory.
  • Retrieving Calcryx: The kobold tribe, led by Yusdrayl, has tasked the party with retrieving their stolen white dragon wyrmling, Calcryx, from the goblins. In exchange for the dragon's safe return, Yusdrayl has promised the party a powerful metallic key or a choice of treasures from her altar.
  • The Outcast and the Enchanted Fruit: Lucas and Arthur are investigating the mystery of the "unnatural apples" and the source of local twig blights. The kobolds identified the antagonist as Belak the Outcast, a rogue druid living in the "Twilight Grove" below who uses the goblins as his servants.

Open Plot Points and Unresolved Threads

  • The Elemental Sapphires: While the party initially valued the five sapphires found by Vlad at 10 gold pieces each, a mysterious voice in Draven’s head (his patron) revealed these stones are actually intended for summoning elementals. The party has yet to act on this information.
  • The "Back Way" to Goblin Territory: The party is currently being guided by Meepo through a "back door" passage that connects the kobold and goblin territories. They have just cleared a magically sealed sanctuary and are poised to move deeper into the goblin-controlled areas of the fortress.
  • The Mystery of "Night Caller": Among the loot recovered from the skeletal sanctuary is a crystal whistle inscribed with the name Night Caller. While identified as a magical item, the party has not yet discovered its specific function.
  • The Missing Key: The party previously encountered a heavy stone and metal door in the southern part of the citadel that they could not open. They are currently looking for a key that fits this lock, which may be the one held in the mouth of the dragon statue behind Yusdrayl’s throne.
  • Lucas’s Business Ventures: Lucas struck a deal with Yusdrayl to provide concentrated poison for the kobold warriors. He has already received 10 gold pieces and two spell scrolls (Knock and Spider Climb) for a portion of the deal, but he may intend to pursue further "business ventures" such as poisoning the goblin water supply.

ITEMS

Currency and Gemstones

Magic and Rare Items

  • Spell Scroll of Knock: Traded by Yusdrayl to Lucas in exchange for concentrated poison. It was immediately used to open the sealed sanctuary door.
  • Spell Scroll of Spider Climb: Also traded by Yusdrayl to Lucas as part of their business arrangement.
  • "Night Caller" (Crystal Whistle): A magic whistle carved from transparent crystal, found on the obsidian altar in the sanctuary. The name is inscribed on it in Dwarvish.
  • Everlasting Candle: A candle of continuous flame that cannot be extinguished. Arthur tied this to his elk horns using a strip of leather to act as a hands-free light source.
  • Potion of Fire Resistance: Found in a crystal flask on the sanctuary altar.
