SUMMARY

The session began in the quiet aftermath of rescuing the gnome acolyte Erky Timbers from the goblin stockades, as the party paused to take a well-earned short rest. Celebrating their accomplishments, the adventurers officially advanced to Level 3. During their downtime, Lucas Aedansbane engaged Arthur Blackthorn and Alejandro Perez in a thoughtful tactical discussion regarding the giant rats infesting the ruins. Lucas questioned whether the goblins were deliberately breeding disease-ridden vermin, prompting Arthur to reflect on natural pestilence and note that subterranean corruption itself might be twisting the local wildlife. Meanwhile, Vlad Laurent successfully navigated the labyrinthine passages of the Sunless Citadel to rejoin his companions.

With their spell slots and hit points restored, the party set off under the guidance of Draven Shadowveil, whose magical hexblade sickle was exerting a persistent, irresistible telepathic tug toward the northeast. Stepping through a heavy archway, the adventurers found themselves in a magnificent, soaring corridor where massive stone pillars were intricately carved with relief sculptures of entwining dragons. Meepo grew visibly ecstatic, sensing that his stolen dragon charge was close at hand. However, as Vlad clanked forward in his heavy plate armor to check a door at the end of the hall, his stealth check failed, alerting a squad of goblin sentries resting nearby.

What followed was a masterclass in tactical party synergy. The goblins surged into the hall to choke the doorway, but Meepo immediately launched a flask of alchemist’s fire straight into their ranks, engulfing multiple sentries in sticky flame. Lucas dipped a crossbow bolt in poison and unleashed a ranged sneak attack for 15 damage. Erky Timbers chimed in with divine support, casting Sacred Flame for 5 damage. Arthur channeled astral power into a Starry Wisp bolt, scoring a spectacular natural 20 critical hit for 12 damage. Closing the trap, Vlad stepped into the fray and swung his great axe in a sweeping cleave attack, rolling a 22 to deal 15 damage and slicing the remaining goblins in half! To secure their perimeter, Lucas scattered a fistful of ball bearings across the floor, creating an instant hazard to trip up any incoming reinforcements.

Pushing forward into an adjacent chamber, Vlad opened a door to reveal a dusty trophy room adorned with crude taxidermy—including mounted cattle heads, giant rats, and severed kobold heads. A biting chill hung in the air, coating the stone flagstones in frost. Resting behind an upturned table was Calcryx, the stolen white dragon wyrmling. Overjoyed, Meepo sprinted across the room crying her name, only for Calcryx to unleash a 15-foot cone of freezing ice breath. Meepo barely survived his Constitution save, taking 11 cold damage and ending up frosted over like an “ice popsicle”.

Recognizing the terrifying threat of an actual dragon, Vlad raised his hands and addressed Calcryx in Draconic, attempting to negotiate her peaceful return. However, Calcryx proudly refused to return to her kobold pet cage and aggressively demanded all of the party’s gold, gemstones, and Vlad’s crystal whistle, Night Caller. Understanding that combat against a dragon was a quick path to a total party wipe, Lucas carefully laid down a looted ruby and a vial of poison, executing a flawless rogue “back-shuffle” out of the room while catching his breath. Draven, Alejandro, and Arthur followed suit, surrendering their gemstones and loose coins to preserve their lives. Vlad reluctantly surrendered Night Caller, 2 GP, and two peridot gems. As Vlad backed out, Calcryx fired a parting blast of frost that froze the stone door completely shut, dealing 5 cold damage to Vlad. Vlad forced the icy door open, scrambled clear, and healed himself with 5 points from his Lay on Hands pool. Once safely in the corridor, Lucas delivered an iconic rant, reminding Vlad that dragons can level entire towns and that surrendering a few gems to survive was the smartest move they could make.

Refusing to let their momentum stall, the adventurers moved to clear the remaining goblin guardposts. Arthur wild-shaped into a massive dire wolf, tearing into the goblin lines with heavy bite attacks. Vlad fought shoulder-to-shoulder with his lupine ally, swinging his great axe in devastating cleave strikes that cleaved through the sentries.

The party then approached the throne room of Chief Durnn, the hobgoblin ruler of the Durbuluk tribe. Lucas took the initiative, using his high Deception skill to pose as a traveling poison courier. Inside the hall, Lucas met the goblin shaman Grenel. Exploiting the visible hatred between Grenel and Chief Durnn, Lucas negotiated a secret truce. Grenel revealed that Calcryx had taken over the trophy room on her own four days ago, eating two goblins in the process. Crucially, Grenel disclosed that when the human adventurers passed through, Chief Durnn got into a violent brawl with Talgen Hucrele, killed him, and took his splint armor as a personal trophy.

Armed with this critical intel, Lucas walked straight up to Chief Durnn and extended a hand to shake on a deal. As Durnn grasped his hand, Lucas executed a masterstroke surprise attack—drawing his rapier in a single fluid stroke and plunging it directly into Durnn’s neck for a massive 25 damage! Lucas then rolled a natural 20 on initiative, seizing complete control of the battle’s opening turn.

The throne room erupted into a fierce, high-stakes boss battle. Draven immediately cast Blur on himself to impose disadvantage on enemy attacks while readying Hellish Rebuke. Alejandro charged forward and slammed his heavy mace into a goblin sentry’s skull with a critical hit for 10 damage. Sensing the threat, a stone pot in the corner sprouted a twig blight—a magical spy serving Belak the Outcast.

Chief Durnn proved to be an absolute juggernaut; he chugged potions of haste and barkskin, boosting his Armor Class to a formidable 21. Durnn retaliated with terrifying power, swinging his longsword (+7 to hit) for 30 damage, cutting down Lucas and leaving him incapacitated. Draven placed his Hexblade’s Curse on Durnn and targeted the advancing twig blight with Chill Touch, dealing 7 damage to destroy the plant spy. Alejandro reloaded his firearm and cast Healing Word on Lucas, bringing the rogue back into the fight. Durnn then downed a potion of healing and struck Alejandro for 27 damage, knocking the cleric unconscious. Turning his wrath on Arthur’s dire wolf form, Durnn dealt 25 damage, battering Arthur back into his human shape at 1 hit point.

With the party teetering on the edge of defeat, Erky Timbers stepped forward and called upon Pelor for divine intervention. Channeling a magnificent wave of holy light, Erky restored 13 HP to Draven, 10 HP to Lucas, 12 HP to Arthur, and 14 HP to Alejandro, instantly raising every fallen hero back to their feet! Erky then aimed his holy symbol at Chief Durnn and cast Sacred Flame, scoring a natural 20 critical hit for 17 radiant damage that forced the mighty hobgoblin chief down to one knee!

Seizing the ultimate opening, Arthur cast Hold Person, forcing Durnn to fail a DC 13 Wisdom save and freezing the chief completely in place. With Durnn paralyzed, Lucas, Draven, and Vlad combined their power into a final, devastating onslaught, dealing over 20 damage to slice through Durnn and slay the hobgoblin chief once and for all!

In the glorious aftermath of their victory, Draven claimed Durnn’s finely crafted +1 longsword to convert into his pact blade. The adventurers recovered Talgen’s splint armor, recognizable by the Hucrele family crest on the breastplate. Grenel then pointed out Durnn’s iron chest near the throne. Inspecting the lock, Lucas spotted a poisoned needle trap and rolled an incredible 23 Dexterity check with his thieves’ tools, disarming the trap cleanly and popping the chest open. Inside, they claimed a massive hoard of 231 gold pieces and two uncut onyx gems. Having eliminated the chief, secured major loot, and cleared the upper fortress level, the victorious party prepared for their eventual descent into the Twilight Grove below.
