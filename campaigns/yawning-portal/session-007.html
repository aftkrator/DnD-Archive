SUMMARY

The session began with the party returning to Yusdrayl, the kobold leader, following their victorious battle against the goblin chief Durnn. They reported that while the goblin chief was dead, the white dragon wyrmling Calcryx refused to return to the kobolds, preferring its new lair and freezing everything in sight. Satisfied that the adventurers had fulfilled their end of the bargain regarding the goblins, Yusdrayl handed over the metallic key to the Dragon Door—which hung above her throne—and bade them farewell. The party debated whether to descend immediately to the lower floor or backtrack to the unopened Dragon Door from earlier sessions. They chose to return to the Dragon Door and resolve its mystery once and for all.

Arriving at the grand marble barrier, Lucas inserted Yusdrayl’s key. The mechanical lock turned with a click, and the heavy door swung open, releasing a puff of ancient dust from a gallery that had been sealed for ages. As they stepped inside, a soft, haunting celestial music began echoing from cracked crystal globes resting on stone pedestals. Draven approached one of the globes and touched it, failing a Wisdom saving throw and becoming magically charmed. Compelled by the orb, Draven grabbed it and began running aimlessly around the room. Seeing his companion entranced, Lucas took careful aim with his rapier and struck the crystal orb in Draven’s hands, shattering it to pieces. The destruction of the orb immediately broke the charm, bringing Draven back to his senses.

Pushing forward into a dark connecting corridor, Draven stepped on a hidden pressure plate, triggering an arrow trap from the ceiling that struck his arm for 5 piercing damage. Lucas quickly investigated the corridor and successfully disarmed the trap mechanism with his thieves’ tools. Moving into the next chamber, the adventurers found themselves facing a 10-foot-tall sculpture of a coiled dragon carved from red-veined white marble. The dragon statue suddenly uncoiled, unfurled its wings, and spoke a riddle in Common: “We come at night without being fetched. We disappear by day without being stolen. What are we?” The party correctly answered “stars,” prompting the statue to step aside and unlock the door beyond.

Beyond the riddle door lay a hall with six alcoves containing red-veined white marble elf statues, ending in an archway with a greenish glow. Lucas carefully inspected the room and spotted a concealed secret door set into the wall. Arthur cast Guidance on himself and wild-shaped into a rat to scout through a tight crawlspace behind the secret door. Navigating the passage, rat-Arthur discovered a chamber featuring a deep pit with wooden stakes and a dragon-shaped stone sarcophagus illuminated by a eerie green flame.

The rest of the party followed Arthur into the sarcophagus room. Finding six heavy iron latches fastening the lid, Vlad applied his immense physical strength, snapping all six latches one by one over multiple attempts. Together, the entire group joined forces to lift the heavy stone lid, revealing an ancient troll dressed in rotted finery and fine jewels—the citadel’s legendary Dragon Priest (a CR 6 encounter).

Combat erupted immediately. Lucas opened with ranged sneak attacks, while Arthur (remaining in rat form) maintained Moonbeam and launched Starry Wisp radiant bolts. Vlad landed a massive strike infused with a Paladin Divine Smite, dealing 31 damage in a single turn. However, the troll struck back savagely with claws and teeth, focusing its fury on Alejandro. The cleric took heavy damage and collapsed to 0 hit points, falling into the pit and beginning his death saving throws. Seeing Alejandro near death, Arthur scampered down into the pit in rat form and cast Cure Wounds, reviving him just in time. Lucas then delivered a devastating critical hit (natural 20) with his bow for 33 damage, putting an arrow straight through the troll’s skull and knocking it to the ground.

To their shock, the troll’s severe wounds began closing as its flesh regenerated, and it stood right back up on its feet. Recognizing that trolls require fire or radiant energy to prevent regeneration, Arthur shifted his Moonbeam directly onto the beast while Lucas and Vlad used fire and coordinated strikes to finish it off permanently.

With the Dragon Priest vanquished, the adventurers thoroughly searched the sarcophagus and room:

  • Vlad took a ceremonial dagger worth 125 GP.
  • Lucas found two silver rings worth 15 GP each.
  • Arthur claimed a silver amulet worth 15 GP.
  • A hidden compartment in the false bottom of the sarcophagus yielded four spell scrolls: Command, Cure Wounds, Inflict Wounds, and Guiding Bolt.
  • The group gathered 220 silver pieces and 72 gold pieces in loose coins.
  • Arthur harvested two vials of troll blood to analyze during future downtime.

Having fully cleared the upper level of the Sunless Citadel and resolved the mystery of the Dragon Door, the Dungeon Master announced that the party had officially advanced from Level 3 to Level 4!
