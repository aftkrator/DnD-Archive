SUMMARY

The session began in the immediate aftermath of the previous skirmish, with the captured goblin sentry pinned to the cold stone floor under the weight of Arthur Blackthorn’s dire wolf form. The DM noted that the prisoner was still being thoroughly harassed by the bag of centipedes Meepo had released upon him. Seizing the initiative, Lucas Aedansbane pressed the cold steel of his rapier against the goblin’s cheek, demanding answers.

The terrified goblin, who introduced himself in Common as Gordo, pleaded to have the centipedes removed. Under Lucas’s intense questioning, Gordo revealed he was a guard tasked with protecting the goblin territory, which stretched from their current location down to the entrance of the “under floor”. When Lucas tried to intimidate him by counting to ten and threatening to chop off a finger for every number, Gordo’s complete lack of numerical literacy became hilariously evident—he simply could not count past ten, claiming there were “10…” goblins in the citadel.

Attempting a “good cop” routine, Lucas slid a single silver piece into Gordo’s pocket and whispered that while the others—including a hungry wolf and a dragonborn—wanted to kill him, Lucas would keep him alive if he cooperated. Gordo remained highly skeptical, pointing out that the party had just killed his sentry partner. Gordo went on to explain that a band of fewer than ten goblins usually patrolled nearby, and that they detested the kobolds for occupying “their” citadel. When asked about a dragon, Gordo scoffed at the idea of goblins stealing one, but noted that their Shaman was deeply obsessed with dragons and lived “inside” with their “big” leader. Crucially, Gordo mentioned hearing talk of three human captives who had passed through a month prior.

The interrogation took a disastrous turn when Lucas remarked that Gordo’s life was sad. Insulted and enraged, Gordo snapped, boasting that his life was full. He threw a fistful of dust and rocks directly into Lucas’s eyes and scrambled out of Arthur’s wolfish grip. Both Arthur and Lucas were forced to make Dexterity saves. Arthur rolled poorly, while Lucas managed a “dirty 20”. Though Lucas was not blinded, he was momentarily taken aback, preventing him from taking an action. Gordo bolted 30 feet down the hallway to alert his allies.

Active combat began as Gordo’s screams alerted two more goblin guards deeper in the corridor. Meepo acted first in the initiative order, lunging forward with his dagger. With a spectacular roll of 22, Meepo stabbed the fleeing Gordo for 6 damage, instantly killing the wounded sentry.

Lucas quickly shook off the dust, used his Cunning Action to Dash, and closed the distance to engage the newly arrived guards. He executed a ranged sneak attack against the northern goblin, rolling a 16 and dealing 16 damage with an arrow straight to the head, killing it instantly. Lucas then slipped into the shadows to Hide. The surviving goblin retaliated by shooting a shortbow at Arthur’s dire wolf form, dealing 8 damage, while a second goblin guard lunged with a shortsword for another 4 damage, leaving the druid badly battered.

Draven Shadowveil retaliated by targeting the top goblin with an Eldritch Blast, dealing 5 damage. Fueled by primal anger, Arthur lunged at the goblin that had shot him. He rolled a 23 to hit, dealing 12 damage as he sank his heavy dire wolf jaws into the goblin’s neck and snapped it. Meepo rushed the final goblin, scoring another roll of 22 with his dagger for 3 damage. Lucas finished the skirmish by throwing his dagger with a Dexterity-based spin throw, rolling a 15 to hit and dealing 7 damage to kill the last standing guard. The DM declared the area clear of goblins, and Lucas successfully retrieved his thrown dagger and rolled a d4 to salvage his arrow unbroken.

In a bizarre and unsettling display of primal instinct, Arthur remained in his dire wolf form and began consuming the dead goblin’s carcass. Lucas watched in deep concern, reminding Arthur that civilized druids do not typically consume sentient flesh. As the personality-altering effects of the wild shape slowly wore off, Arthur licked his jaws, sat down, and telepathically confessed to Lucas, “I don’t know what came over me”. Lucas dryly advised him that eating goblins was unhygienic, adding a warning to never “shag” one, as he had seen many men die from that very mistake.

Taking a moment to rest, Arthur opened up to Lucas about his past, admitting that he did not actually belong to the druidic circle he represented but was executing this mission as a favor to repay them for years of shelter. The quiet moment was interrupted when they realized their paladin companion, Vlad Laurent, had mysteriously vanished from their ranks.

Suddenly, a faint, metallic rattling and a muffled voice echoed from a nearby door. Arthur used his heightened wolf senses to listen closely, determining that the noise was coming from a single, weak creature. The air near the door smelled intensely foul; Draven noted with disgust that it smelled exactly like the Thayan prison cell he had escaped from.

The party entered the room and discovered the goblin stockade. Inside a cramped cage was a gagged gnome. Lucas removed the gag, introducing Erky Timbers, a gnome acolyte of Pelor. While Lucas worked on picking the cage lock, Erky explained that he had been captured by goblin bandits on the Old Road months ago and had only survived starvation thanks to his deity’s blessings.

Erky delivered a massive revelation: a month ago, the goblins had kept three human captives—Talgen, Sharwyn, and Sir Braford—in the stockades for a week before moving them. Erky had overheard the goblins whispering that a wicked old druid named Belak wanted them down in the Twilight Grove below, where he tended an enchanted garden and harvested magical solstice fruit from the Gulthias Tree. The DM noted that Talgen and Sharwyn were the missing children of Kerowyn Hucrele, and Sir Braford was Vlad’s lost paladin friend.

With the cage lock successfully clicked open, Erky gratefully joined the party as a temporary ally, providing them with much-needed healing capabilities. Before leaving, Meepo noticed another set of cells containing three of his fellow kobolds chained to the wall. Arthur helped untie them while Meepo dissolved their manacles with acid. The rescued kobolds thanked the party and were directed by Lucas back toward safe kobold territory. Lucas pocketed the rusted iron manacles that had bound Erky, and with their primary quest objectives finally clarified, the adventurers prepared to descend into the Twilight Grove.
